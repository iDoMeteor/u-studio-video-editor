# Captions. Voice cedar.

## intro
In this video, we'll add captions to our edit: import a subtitle file, see it play, fix a line, and export the captions again.

## Import captions
Import a subtitle file, an S R T or a V T T, just like any other media. Every line becomes a caption, at its time, on a new Captions track at the top.

## Captions play over the video
The captions play right over the video. Italics and colours from the file come through, and a caption the file places at the top of the picture goes at the top.

## Fix a caption
Spotted a typo? Select the caption, and correct its words on the Title page. The timeline names each caption by its first line.

## Export captions
When you're done, Export Captions writes every caption, with your fixes, as an S R T or a V T T file, ready for YouTube or any web player.

## outro
That's captions in U Stu Video Editor. Thanks for watching!
