# Effects 1: the Add page, trying effects, the Effects page, Looks, Compare. Voice cedar.

## intro
Effects change how your clips look: glows, colour, blur, and much more. In this video, we'll find an effect, try it on the picture, add it, and compare before and after.

## The Add page
Select a clip and press E. The Add page shows a tile for every effect, each one showing your own frame with that effect on it. The badge says how much work it is to play.

## Search and try
Type to search: glow. Now just point at a tile, and the picture shows your clip with that effect, without changing anything yet.

## Add an effect
Found the one you want? Click the tile to add it.

## The Effects page
The Effects page lists what's on the clip, with every setting, and a Mix slider that blends the effect with the original picture.

## Looks
Looks are ready-made sets of effects. The brand looks, like Neon Night, come with U Stu, and you can save your own.

## Before and after
And Compare splits the picture: before on the left, after on the right, even while it plays.

## outro
That's effects in U Stu Video Editor. Next, we'll look at transitions. Thanks for watching!
