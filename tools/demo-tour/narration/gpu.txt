# GPU acceleration, recorded on the owner's real desktop (Intel Iris Xe). Voice cedar.

## intro
GPU acceleration lets your graphics card do the heavy lifting: placing, scaling and blending your tracks. This part was recorded on a real desktop, on a laptop with Intel graphics.

## GPU acceleration on
Here's a three-track project at full size: a background, with two pictures scaled and moved on top. With GPU acceleration on, the graphics card composites every frame.

## Turning it off
GPU acceleration lives in Settings, on the Performance page, under Hardware, and the row shows which graphics card it's using. For comparison, let's switch it off.

## GPU acceleration off
Now the processor does all the work.

## outro
On this laptop, the same three tracks played at about twenty-six frames a second with the graphics card, and about twenty without it, and the editor used less of the processor. Thanks for watching!
