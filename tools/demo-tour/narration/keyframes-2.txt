# Keyframed transforms. Voice cedar.

## intro
In version zero point eight, a picture's position, size and rotation can change over time. Let's make a picture-in-picture glide in, grow and straighten up.

## The Transform card
Select a clip, and the Effects page now starts with a Transform card: its position, width, height and rotation.

## Pin a starting point
Set where it should start, small and tilted in the corner, then click the star beside each value to pin it at the playhead.

## Change it later
Move the playhead on, and change the values again. They're pinned there automatically.

## It moves
Play it, and the picture glides between the two, growing and straightening as it goes.

## Drag on the preview
You can shape it on the picture too. On a keyframed clip, dragging it on the preview sets a keyframe at the playhead, just for the values you moved.

## Curve lanes
Press C to see the curves: the transform's lanes come first, with a dot for every keyframe, ready to drag.

## Touch-record
Or perform it. Arm touch-record, play, and move the value as it plays. U Stu keeps just enough keyframes to follow what you did.

## outro
That's keyframed transforms in U Stu Video Editor. Thanks for watching!
