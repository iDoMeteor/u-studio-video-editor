# Picture in picture. Voice cedar.

## intro
In this video, we'll put one picture inside another: scale it, move it, crop it, rotate it and flip it, right on the preview.

## Transform on the preview
Click a picture in the preview to select it. Drag a corner to scale it, and drag the middle to move it. Guides appear, and snap it into line with the frame's centre and edges.

## Crop (Alt+drag a side)
Hold Alt and drag a side handle to crop the picture, instead of squashing it.

## Rotate
Drag the round handle above the box to rotate the picture. Hold Shift to turn it in fifteen-degree steps.

## Transform menu
Right-click the picture for quick moves: fit or stretch it to the frame, centre it, flip it, or give it a quarter turn.

## Edit Transform
And Control T shows the exact numbers beside the preview, for pixel-perfect placement. Every change is a single undo step.
