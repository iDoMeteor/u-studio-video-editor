# Settings and Help. Voice cedar.

## intro
This video is a quick tour of the settings, and of the help that's built into U Stu Video Editor.

## Settings
Settings has every preference in one place, grouped into pages.

## Settings: Toggles
Toggles switches everyday behaviours on and off: reopening your last project, snapping, following the playhead, thumbnails and waveforms.

## Settings: Performance
Performance is where you tune the preview, proxies for heavy footage, graphics acceleration and hardware video decoding, and how much work runs in the background.

## Settings: Locations
Locations sets where your projects are saved, and where finished videos go.

## Settings: Render
Render holds your render profiles. High quality is the default, and you can make your own.

## Settings: Drop-ins
Drop-ins lists the optional add-ons, like titles and effects, with a switch for each one. U Stu never downloads anything; add-ons are installed like any other package.

## Settings: Shortcuts
And Shortcuts is where you'll soon be able to change the keys. For now, Help lists every shortcut.

## Help
Help explains every control and lists every shortcut, in sections that remember where you were.

## Release notes
Release notes show what's new in each version, written for the people testing it.

## About and diagnostics
And About has the version details. If something goes wrong, Copy Diagnostics puts everything a bug report needs on your clipboard.

## outro
That's settings and help. Thanks for watching!
