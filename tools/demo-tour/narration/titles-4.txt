# Titles 4: animated layers. Voice cedar.

## intro
Titles can hold ready-made animations too: a logo sting, a sticker, or an icon that moves. In this video, we'll add one.

## Animated templates
Two built-in templates show how it looks: this lower third has a bell that rings every few seconds. The bell is an animated layer inside the title.

## Add an animation
To add your own, open the plus menu and choose Animation, then pick a Lottie file, the kind LottieFiles and After Effects export. It lands in the middle, and you move and size it like a picture. It stays sharp at any size.

## Plays and Speed
In the inspector, Plays repeats the animation over and over, or plays it once and holds the last frame. And Speed makes it faster or slower.

## In the editor
Save, and the editor plays the animation too, exactly as the designer does, and so does your export.

## outro
That's animated layers in U Stu Titles. Thanks for watching!
