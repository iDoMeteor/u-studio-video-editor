# Transitions 2: tiles with your clips, zoom, spin, and the sound's crossfade. Voice cedar.

## intro
There's more to transitions in version zero point seven nine: new zoom and spin styles, tiles that show your own footage, and a better crossfade for music.

## Your own clips on the tiles
Press T at a cut, and the Transitions page opens. Every tile now shows your own two clips, half-way through that style, so you can see what you'll get before you click.

## Zoom
Zoom In grows the next clip in from the middle.

## Spin
And Spin In turns it as it grows, clockwise or the other way.

## Sound: equal power
The sound crosses too. Even crossfade is the default; Equal power keeps the level up through the middle, which sounds better for music and ambience. It stays when you change the style.

## outro
That's what's new in transitions. Thanks for watching!
