# Transitions 3: blend dissolves and Sound: Cut. Voice cedar.

## intro
Version zero point eight adds three new dissolves that blend the two clips, and a new way for the sound to change over.

## Blend dissolves
Press T at a cut, and try the blend dissolves. Additive adds the incoming clip over the outgoing one, for a bright, punchy change.

## Screen
Screen blends the two softly, with a gentle glow half-way through.

## Lighten
And Lighten shows whichever picture is lighter at every point.

## Sound: Cut
Under Sound, Cut plays the first clip's sound up to the middle of the transition, and the second clip's from there, with no blend. It's ideal for dialogue, or cutting on a beat.

## outro
That's what's new in transitions. Thanks for watching!
