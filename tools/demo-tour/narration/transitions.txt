# Transitions. Voice cedar.

## intro
Transitions carry your viewer from one clip to the next. In this video, we'll add one with a single key, and try the different styles.

## Press T at a cut
Put the playhead near a cut and press T. A short dissolve appears at the cut, and the Transitions page opens beside the picture.

## Transition styles
Each tile shows its style, with the outgoing clip in pink and the incoming one in blue. Click one, and the dissolve plays that way; here, a dip to black.

## Wipes
There are twenty wipes, from simple edges to a circle, a clock, and even a unicorn horn.

## Softness and reverse
A wipe's edge can be soft or sharp, and Reverse runs it the other way. Every change is a single undo step.

## Slides and pushes
And the motion styles slide the next clip in over this one, or push it out, from any side.

## outro
That's transitions in U Stu Video Editor. Thanks for watching!
