# What's new in 0.78: a highlight reel for testers. Voice cedar.

## intro
Here's what's new in U Stu Video Editor zero point seven eight, in under three minutes. Everything you see is in this beta, ready to try.

## Effects
Effects are here. Select a clip, press E, and just point at a tile to preview it on your own picture. Click to add it.

## Before and after
Compare splits the picture, before on the left and after on the right, even while it plays.

## Transitions
Press T at a cut for a transition, and pick a style: dissolves, wipes, slides, and now zoom and spin.

## Animated titles
Titles can now hold animations, like this star from a Lottie file, and some templates come with a ringing bell.

## Title templates
The template gallery has lower thirds, badges, cards and countdowns, ready for streams and social posts.

## Captions
Import subtitle files as captions, fix them in place, and export them again for YouTube.

## GPU acceleration
And GPU acceleration hands the heavy lifting to your graphics card. On our test laptop, playback went from about twenty to about twenty-six frames a second.

## outro
Please try them out, and tell us what you find. Copy Diagnostics in Help gathers what a bug report needs. Thanks for testing!
